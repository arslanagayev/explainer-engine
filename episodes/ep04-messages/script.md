## Voice-over

How can nobody read your WhatsApp messages? Not even WhatsApp?
Here's the twist: every message goes through their servers.
But what arrives there is scrambled, and they don't have the key.
Your phone makes two keys. A public one it shares, and a private one that never leaves it.
Think of the public key as an open padlock. Anyone can snap it shut.
But only the private key can open it again.
So when you message a friend, your phone locks it with your friend's padlock.
The server only sees a locked box. It just passes it on.
Your friend's phone opens it with the key it never shared.
But what if a key is stolen one day?
So the keys keep changing.
Every message gets a fresh key, and old ones are thrown away.
Stealing today's key doesn't unlock yesterday's chats.
Want proof nobody swapped the padlock in between?
Compare the security code with your friend. A match means nobody's in the middle.
One honest catch: the server still sees who you talk to, and when.
But what you say stays between your two phones.
Now you know.
